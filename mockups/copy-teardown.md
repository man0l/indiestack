# Copy teardown — the products your landing page sells against

Fetched live 2026-09-02. The frame, not the whole copy: headline, subhead, promise, audience, mechanism, before→after, offer, guarantee, proof, FAQ, and the framework a copywriter would label it with.

Products named in `mockups/landing.html`: UptimeRobot, Better Stack, Cronitor, Instatus, Pingdom, Checkly, Healthchecks.io, Uptime Kuma (+ "skip" mentions Datadog, PagerDuty).

## Part 1 — Per-product funnel breakdown

### 1. UptimeRobot — the free-tier incumbent your copy attacks

| Component | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Headline** | "Meet the #1 uptime monitoring service" |
| **Subhead** | Bullets, not a sentence: "50 monitors for free · 20+ integrations · Real-time alerts, web & mobile · Public status pages" |
| **Framework** | **Free-offer-led PLG + category claim.** AIDA-flavored, zero PAS — the problem is never stated; the giveaway IS the message. |
| **Audience** | Self-serve indies, webmasters, small agencies — people paying out of pocket. |
| **Problem** | Implicit only: "Start monitoring in 30 seconds" = you currently have nothing running. |
| **Promise** | You'll know instantly, and it costs nothing to start. |
| **Mechanism** | 50 free monitors, 20+ integrations, SSL/keyword/cron monitors, status pages, "#1" claim. |
| **Before → After** | Refreshing your site manually / finding out from customers → alerted in Slack in 30 seconds of setup. Calm, and free. |
| **Offer** | Free forever: 50 monitors, all Pro features at 5-min checks. Paid: Solo ~$8/mo annual, Team ~$34/mo annual, Enterprise ~$80/mo. |
| **Guarantee** | Free tier as trial. "No credit card required" (stated twice). |
| **Proof** | The "#1" claim as headline; "trusted by millions" family; small-SaaS logo wall. |
| **FAQ** | What counts as a monitor, free vs PRO ("5x faster detection"), billing mechanics, channels, intervals. |
| **Steal** | The blunt "no credit card" micro-copy next to the CTA. |

## Part 2 — The frames in play (pattern analysis)

| Frame | Who | Emotional register | Why it works | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free-offer-led PLG | UptimeRobot, Instatus, Checkly, Cronitor (free tier) | Zero-risk bargain | No decision to make; the product sells itself | Attracts freebie traffic; paid conversion is the hard part |
| Category claim / superlative | UR ("#1"), Better Stack ("most reliable"), Checkly ("active reliability layer"), Datadog | Confidence, authority | Positions before the compare-tab opens | Claims need proof to survive a skeptical dev |
| Challenger price-positioning | Better Stack ("30x cheaper than Datadog") | Smug relief | Names the incumbent's bill as the villain | Needs a believable price gap |
| Identity claim | Cronitor ("made for developers"), Uptime Kuma (self-hosters) | Belonging, flattery | Devs buy from tools that name them | Thin if the product doesn't back it |
| Problem-first (PAS) | Healthchecks (calm), PagerDuty (urgent) | Fear → relief | The pain is universal and visceral | Over-agitation reads as marketing |
| Preparedness frame | Instatus ("Get ready for downtime") | Calm control | Normalizes the threat instead of scaremongering | Subtler hook |
| Artifact-as-landing | Uptime Kuma (Docker cmd), Cronitor (live dashboard), IndieStack's status mock | Show-don't-tell | Devs trust working artifacts over adjectives | Needs the artifact to actually be good |

**The category's universal problem frame** — worth underlining: nobody sells "uptime" in the H1; everyone sells the *silent failure*: "failures don't stay silent" (Cronitor), "can go unnoticed for a long time" (Healthchecks), "No human could ever keep track…" (Pingdom's testimonial). Downtime is the fear; **not knowing** is the pain.

**Risk-reversal spectrum** (weakest → strongest): trial w/ card (Pingdom) → free tier (most) → "no credit card" (UR, Instatus) → cancel anytime (Cronitor) → **60-day money-back (Better Stack — the benchmark)** → license-as-reversal (Kuma). IndieStack currently sits at **zero** — pay first, no stated refund.

**Proof spectrum:** big counts (PagerDuty "70% of Fortune 100", UR "millions", Better Stack "7,000+", Kuma "90,865 stars") → named peer testimonials (Healthchecks) → live artifact (Cronitor, Kuma) → nothing (Instatus homepage, Cronitor pages). IndieStack's "See the live Worker" = artifact-as-proof, the right pattern — but it's the *only* proof on the page.

## Part 3 — Pricing reality check vs the IndieStack receipt

The landing cites "CIVCI prices, not a made-up bundle." Live check, Sep 2026:

| Landing claim | Current reality (vendor pages) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Better Stack / UR paid **$408/yr** ($34/mo) | UR Team still ~$34/mo = $408 ✓ · **Better Stack paid uptime from $21/mo yearly ($25/mo monthly) = $252/yr**; 10 monitors free; the "$29/mo" is the Responder (Uptime+Telemetry) *seat*, not the uptime product | Half right — BS's $29 was the seat price |
| Cronitor **$120/yr** ($10/mo) | **Flat pricing gone.** Now $2/mo per monitor + $5/mo per user → 5 monitors + 1 user = $15/mo = $180/yr; their own example: 20 mon + 3 users = $55/mo | Stale — the $10/mo flat number no longer exists |
| Pingdom **$180** | $16.50/mo annual ≈ **$198** | Close, slightly stale |
| Checkly **$288** | Starter $24/mo annual = **$288** ✓ | Exact match |
| Instatus status pages "~$29/mo" | Pro **$15–20/mo** ($180–240/yr); $300/mo is the next tier | Stale — Instatus got cheaper, which *weakens* the status-page pole |

## Part 4 — What this means for IndieStack's copy (funnel-component audit)

| Funnel component | The category norm | IndieStack today | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Audience** | Teams/roles (SRE, IT, marketers); Cronitor = "developers"; nobody owns "indie" | "Indie" identity throughout — **unoccupied frame, keep** | Double down: name the indie's stack (side project + cron + VPS) |
| **Problem** | Silent failure ("failures don't stay silent") | Financial pain ($528, paywall) — cost-villain PAS | Add the emotional pole: the backup that died Tuesday unnoticed. Cost is the *agitate*, silent failure is the *problem* |
| **Promise** | Know first / stay calm / contain small failures | "Kill the $528/year stack" — a promise about money, not sleep | Pair them: "Discord when it dies. $0/mo after." |
| **Mechanism** | Monitors + heartbeats + status page (table stakes everywhere now) | Same trio + "Worker not on your origin" (the Kuma-trap argument) | The strongest mechanism claim you have; it's currently mid-page |
| **Transformation** | Chaos→calm (emotional) | Paying→not-paying (financial) | State both: before = silent dead backup + $44/mo; after = Discord ping + $0/mo |
| **Offer** | Free tier → usage pricing → enterprise; everyone recurring | **Only one-time purchase in the category** — un-copyable by SaaS | Lead harder with "once"; nobody else can say it |
| **Guarantee** | No-CC free tier standard; Better Stack's 60-day money-back = benchmark | **None stated** — the biggest gap | 14–30 day "keep the repo" refund near the button |
| **Proof** | Counts, peer testimonials, or live artifact | Live Worker only | Add one real Discord alert screenshot + one maker testimonial (Healthchecks pattern) |
| **FAQ** | Billing mechanics + head-on competitor Qs | Strong: vibecode-it, Kuma, free-plan | Add "what exactly do I get" + refund FAQ (Checkly/Healthchecks patterns) |
| **Headline frame** | Category claim, identity claim, or free offer — fear never leads | Problem-story ("Free monitoring ended when you started charging") | Keep the villain, but get the product/outcome into the H1 (the "strong" iteration did) |
| **CTA** | "Start free" everywhere — PLG verbs | "Skip the paywall / Cancel $528" — money verbs, one action | Unify one verb family; micro-copy under button: "One-time · keep the repo · no subscription" |

**Top 5 steals, ranked:**
1. **Risk reversal** — a "keep the repo" refund window (Better Stack's 60-day is the norm-setter; match the *mechanism*, not the length).
2. **The silent-failure problem frame** — borrow Healthchecks' line-shape ("a failed backup can go unnoticed for a long time") as the before-state.
3. **Artifact + one peer quote** — you already have the artifact (live Worker); add a named indie testimonial the way Healthchecks does.
4. **Head-on FAQ** — you have Kuma/vibecode; add "Why not just Healthchecks.io free + UptimeRobot free?" because that's the real free-stack objection your page invites.
5. **Refresh the receipt** — Cronitor's flat $10/mo is gone and Instatus got cheaper; Better Stack's paid uptime is $21/mo yearly, so the verified pair is $252 + $180 = $432 — the page is now re-anchored to that.
