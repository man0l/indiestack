The mechanism: a Worker that isn’t on your origin.

If the monitor shares the box with the app, it dies with the app — that’s the Kuma trap. IndieStack runs on Cloudflare, not on the VPS it watches. You deploy it to the account you already have. D1, R2, one cron. The Worker is the monitor.

01
ping

Is the site up?

HTTP, TCP, DNS, SSL and domain expiry. Keyword present/absent, expected status, slow-response ceiling. Two failures before a down alert — one blip is not an outage.

02
heartbeat

Did the job run?

Your cron hits /beat/<token>. If it goes quiet past interval + grace, your channel fires. IndieStack does not run the job. It notices the job did not run.

03
status + logs

Where do I send people?

Public status is / on your Worker. Errors POST to /log/<token> and live 24h in R2 — admin only, never on the status page.

Questions builders actually ask

Is this a hosted product?

No. You deploy the Worker to your Cloudflare account. IndieStack never sees your URLs, and your data never leaves your account. The $120 is for the stack and its updates, not for us to run it.

Will I blow the free plan?

A typical indie — 10 URLs every 5 minutes, a few heartbeats — is a few thousand Worker requests and D1 writes per day against a 100k/day cap of each. Analytics is separately capped at 2,000 views/site/day. The caps exist so you cannot accidentally become a monitoring farm.

Why $120 if Cloudflare is free?

Cloudflare is free. The weekend you’d spend rebuilding UptimeRobot + Cronitor + a status page + analytics is not. Cronitor alone is usage-priced now; this is once. Pay, deploy, cancel the rest.

“Pay once” — what does that actually buy?

The repo and every future update to it. Pull, redeploy, done — the same one-button deploy as day one. What you never get is a bill.

Can I just vibecode this myself?

Yes — that’s the whole canivibecodeit.com point, and the Workers AI age says go for it. IndieStack is for the weekend you’d rather ship the product you’re actually building.

What about Uptime Kuma / Healthchecks?

Kuma is great if you want to babysit a VPS — IndieStack is the same job with no box to patch, on infra you already have. Healthchecks is honest cron monitoring, but it’s still another vendor holding the data, and it doesn’t watch your sites, deploys, logs or traffic.

How does the AI part work — do I need a subscription?

No. The stack serves /agents.md and an MCP endpoint; you mint a token in /admin and point your own Claude, Cursor, or script at it. If your agent needs a subscription, that’s between you and your agent. We ship the access, not the upsell.
